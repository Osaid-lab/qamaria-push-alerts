🍏 iPhone User Setup Required:
To allow background pop-up alerts on Apple devices:
  1. Tap the Share button (box with upward arrow) at the bottom.
  2. Select "Add to Home Screen" from the menu.
  3. Open the newly added app icon on your desktop to track your order!
